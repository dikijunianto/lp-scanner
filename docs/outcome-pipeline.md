# Outcome pipeline

Sprint 6 evaluates due outcomes before live fees and cold enrichment. Due rows move from `PENDING` to `READY`, are claimed as `RUNNING`, and finish as `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNAVAILABLE`. Repeated errors become `FAILED`; interrupted jobs return to `READY` after five minutes. `OUTCOME_JOBS_PER_CYCLE` and `OUTCOME_WORKER_CONCURRENCY` bound work. SQLite evaluation reads saved snapshots and fee aggregates covered by either the historical or live contiguous cursor; no external API is required. The JavaScript tasks yield between jobs so the foreground scanner can proceed, while SQLite remains a single writer.

Jobs near their due time run first. Remaining capacity drains older HIGH/MEDIUM research signals, with 4h/24h maturity favored. Every new episode schedules 30m, 1h, 4h and 24h. Existing episodes and outcomes are preserved. An outcome keeps usable fields even if other fields are missing; `outcomeCompletenessPct` and normalized missing reasons record which evidence is absent. `COMPLETE` still requires an endpoint, valid price return and at least 80% observed time coverage. Sparse 4h/24h history therefore remains partial or unavailable rather than becoming a fabricated complete outcome.

The diagnostic page separates eligible, complete, partial, unavailable and overdue jobs by horizon. Completion lag is measured as `completedAt - dueAt`, with median and p95. A completed job can still have missing fee/depth fields; inspect field completeness before interpreting a cohort. Targets are 30m <5 minutes late, 1h <10, 4h <15 and 24h <30 under continuous operation. Laptop sleep and snapshot gaps can prevent those targets.
