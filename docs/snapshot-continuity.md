# Core snapshot continuity

`pnpm start` includes a separate `core-writer` process. It writes one compact row per minute for HOT, watched, or active-signal pools. The writer reads current pool state from SQLite and has no network calls or dependency on depth, fee, price, or outcome workers. It stores timestamp, source-update time, price and confidence, activity, risk, 1-hour volume and fees, active liquidity, ±5% depth, and volatility. A pool state older than five minutes yields unavailable measurements rather than a fabricated price.

Full JSON pool snapshots now follow a tiered cadence: at most one every five minutes for HOT, watched, or active-signal pools; hourly for colder pools. Current pool rows still update on every successful discovery scan. Existing full history is untouched. Outcomes merge compact and full history. Price return, range survival, and adverse/favorable moves can complete from compact price observations when fee and depth fields are absent. Each outcome records completeness for price, range, fee, depth, and liquidity separately; `overallCompletenessPct` averages those groups.

The outcome worker also re-evaluates a bounded number of older 4-hour and 24-hour partial records from saved history after current due jobs. A failed replay keeps its prior partial or unavailable status, and repeated failures stop after the normal attempt limit. Old missing observations are not invented; early replays can therefore remain partial.

The writer also opens and closes tracking intervals as pools enter or leave HOT/watched/active status. This interval evidence begins with migration `0008`; earlier compact rows remain available for outcomes but do not make a continuity claim. Data health counts expected minute slots only inside tracked intervals, then reports written rows, reliable-price rows, coverage percentage, largest gap, and pool-level details over the recent four-hour window. A delayed writer leaves a gap inside an open interval; a cold period does not. Missing or stale observations remain unavailable. The readiness gate requires four hours of continuous current tracking with at least 95% row and reliable-price coverage. Pre-Sprint-7 gaps cannot be reconstructed as observations.
