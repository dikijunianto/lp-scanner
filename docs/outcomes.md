# Outcome methodology

Outcomes use **stored post-signal snapshots**. Each 30m/1h/4h/24h horizon waits through `OUTCOME_MAX_GAP_SECONDS` for a snapshot at or after its deadline. A result is complete only with an endpoint and at least 80% observation coverage. Coverage is the sum of consecutive observed time gaps, each capped at the configured maximum, divided by horizon duration. Large collection gaps become partial or unavailable; no missing path is interpolated into a market path.

`priceReturn = endpointPrice / signalPrice − 1`. Maximum up/down moves are the extrema of this same ratio over observed snapshots. `activeLiquidityChange` and `depth5PctChange` use the equivalent endpoint/start ratio, only when both values exist. `riskChange = endpointRisk − signalRisk`. Fee and volume generated between signal and deadline come from confirmed, priced EVM swap events only when the cursor covers the whole interval. Solana generated fees remain null until a comparable event history exists. Post-signal fee efficiency and volume/depth are the measured endpoint metrics; they are not reconstructed from a hypothetical position.

Activity persistence is the fraction of eligible post-signal snapshots at or before the deadline with 1h fee **and/or** volume activity at least `ACTIVITY_PERSISTENCE_FRACTION` times the respective signal-time level. The separate fee and volume persistence fields use their own available measurements. A base level of zero or missing is not evidence of persistence. Missing measures do not enter their denominator; all-missing or insufficient observation coverage yields null.

Historical range outcome compares each observed price with bands centered on `priceAtSignal`: ±2.5%, ±5%, and ±10%. It records whether any observed price exits, time to first observed exit, entry-to-exit transitions, and observed time inside the band. Time segments are capped by the maximum observation gap. An exit between observations may be missed; an exit first seen after the deadline is only approximate. These are **historical range outcomes, not simulated future returns or realized LP profit**. Fees, impermanent loss, execution cost, range rebalancing, and liquidity positioning are not modeled.
