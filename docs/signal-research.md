# Signal research

Signals are descriptive observations, not investment recommendations. The scanner records six types: activity surge, high 1h fee/active-liquidity ratio, high 1h volume/±5% depth ratio, low-risk high-activity, ±1h price breakout, and 30m active-liquidity collapse. Thresholds are in `.env.example`. Fee/depth/liquidity signals require measured values and adequate confidence. Missing data never passes a rule.

One `(pool, signal type)` episode stays active while its rule remains true. Each scan updates last-seen and peak score; the first scan where the rule is false closes it. A later recurrence creates a new episode. Outcomes are scheduled once per episode for +30m, +1h, +4h, and +24h. Existing alert cooldown still applies; repeated scans do not create repeated signal alerts. Each episode stores its signal-time snapshot, reasons, and scanner/activity/risk/rule version strings, so later algorithm changes do not silently rewrite history.

Price coverage uses independent timestamped DefiLlama prices and timestamped CoinGecko fallback. Previously persisted independent observations can bridge a short source outage only while still within their publication-age limit. Discovery quotes have no publication timestamp and remain low-confidence context; they cannot become independent price evidence or veto a fresh independent price. A disagreement with a discovery quote is still flagged. For usable sources, `medianPrice` is the median USD quote and `maxDeviationPct = 100 × max(|sourcePrice/medianPrice − 1|)`. Two fresh sources within the high deviation threshold are high confidence; one reliable source or modest disagreement is medium; stale or conflicting independent prices are unavailable for reliable valuation. The pool UI shows source count, consensus confidence, and disagreement.

Priority tiers use watch state, recent volume, prior surge, and TVL. Tier 1 drives expensive active-liquidity/depth refresh and faster historical fee backfill. This is a resource scheduler, not a pool recommendation. The `/research/coverage` page shows which chains still lack price, fee, or depth data; `/research/signals` and `/research/summary` show historical observations and cohort summaries. Summary medians exclude null values and should be read with their cohort counts and coverage, not as expected returns.
